# Source logos

**Rule: uas never recolors, tints, crops or redraws a logo.** A source's logo is shown only as the file its
owner publishes, unchanged, in its own colors (the owner's dark-background variant in the dark theme, where
there is one). `logos.go` draws these files as SVG images, which no theme, tint or high-contrast mode
recolors. Where a source's trademark policy doesn't clearly allow its unaltered logo for labeling where an app
comes from, or the source has no logo, uas shows a neutral [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) icon in the source's
color instead.

The marks are trademarks of their owners. uas uses them only to say where an app comes from; it is not
affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

  policy](https://www.debian.org/trademark) allows use without asking to "make true factual statements about
  Debian", and "Any scaling must retain the original proportions of the logo." The Debian Open Use Logo is
  Copyright (c) 1999 Software in the Public Interest, Inc., and is released under the terms of the GNU Lesser
  General Public License, version 3 or any later version, or, at your option, of the Creative Commons
  Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported License. Debian is a registered trademark owned by Software in the
  Public Interest, Inc.
- **GitHub:** `github-black.svg` (light backgrounds) and `github-white.svg` (dark backgrounds) are the
  Invertocat from GitHub's [logo kit](https://brand.github.com/GitHub_Logos.zip), unchanged. GitHub's [logo
  guidelines](https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo) list "Show integration: Use a permitted GitHub logo
  to inform others that your project integrates with GitHub" and "Link to GitHub", and forbid changing "the
  color, dimensions" or implying affiliation. GitHub and the Invertocat logo are trademarks of GitHub, Inc.
- **Codeberg:** `codeberg-blue.svg` (light backgrounds) and `codeberg-white.svg` (dark backgrounds) are the

  trademarks of Codeberg e.V.

## Neutral icons instead of logos

- **Fedora** (package): the [Fedora trademark guidelines](https://docs.fedoraproject.org/en-US/legal/trademarks/)
  permit use without permission "only provided that: the use is only in connection with promoting the Fedora
  Project or Fedora code, documentation, content, fonts, and firmware" and "the Fedora brand standards are
  strictly observed". A source label in another app's store isn't one of the uses they describe.
- **Ubuntu** (package) and **Snap** (box): Canonical's [IP rights
  policy](https://ubuntu.com/legal/intellectual-property-policy) says "You can use the Trademarks, in
  accordance with Canonical's brand guidelines, with Canonical's permission in writing." UBUNTU and
  SNAPCRAFT are Canonical trademarks "registered in word and logo form"
  ([list](https://ubuntu.com/legal/trademarks)).
- **Arch Linux** (package): the [Arch Linux trademark
  policy](https://terms.archlinux.org/docs/trademark-policy/) allows non-commercial use that refers to Arch,
  but "the Trademark declaration ( ™ ) must remain intact whenever displaying the logos". The [official
  logos](https://archlinux.org/art/) are the full logo with the "arch linux" wordmark, and cutting the icon
  out of it would alter it.
- **Flathub** (store) and **Homebrew** (terminal): neither publishes a trademark or logo-use policy. Flathub's
  art is CC0, which waives no trademark rights, so their use here isn't clearly allowed.
- **COPR** (hammer) and **AppImage** (file box) have no logo.
